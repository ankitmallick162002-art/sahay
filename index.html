    Prototype notice

    Legal & privacy

    SAHAY is currently a design and engineering prototype, not a live public product. The items below describe the intended direction rather than finished legal documents.

    Privacy Policy

    A full privacy policy has not been published yet. In this prototype, SAHAY stores only what you enter (sign-in identifier, saved resources, deadlines, preferences and feedback) locally in your browser — nothing is sent to a SAHAY server, because no server currently exists for this demo.

    Terms of Use

    Formal terms of use have not been published yet. This prototype is provided for demonstration purposes and should not be relied on for real decisions.

    Accessibility Statement

    SAHAY aims to follow common accessibility practices (keyboard navigation, visible focus, semantic structure, reduced-motion and high-contrast controls) but has not undergone a formal, independent accessibility audit.

    Disclaimer

    SAHAY is an independent information and navigation platform. It does not represent any government agency, and does not replace doctors, lawyers, financial professionals, government authorities or emergency services. Always verify important information with an official or qualified source before acting on it.
